PRIVACY POLICY

APP: NOVEL QR
DEVELOPER: NOVELAR

1. ZERO NETWORK EXPOSURE (NO INTERNET PERMISSION)

Novel QR operates with complete offline isolation. The Application does NOT request, possess, or utilize the android.permission.INTERNET permission. It is architecturally and physically incapable of transmitting any data across the internet.

2. LOCAL CAMERA SENSOR USAGE (ZERO STORAGE & ZERO CLOUD)

The camera permission is utilized exclusively for real-time optical barcode frame analysis directly on-device. Camera frames are processed strictly in volatile device memory (RAM) and are discarded instantly. The Application NEVER records, saves, stores, or transmits video streams, photos, or biometric sensory data to any internal storage or external cloud servers.

3. ZERO PERSONAL DATA COLLECTION (NO ACCOUNTS & NO TRACKING)

The Application requires ZERO user registration, accounts, or sign-ins. We do NOT collect, harvest, store, or monitor any personal information whatsoever, including but not limited to:

  • User names or identity credentials.
  • Email addresses or contact lists.
  • Precise or approximate GPS geolocation data.
  • Device identifiers (IMEI, Android ID, MAC address).
  • Analytics, telemetry, crash trackers, or behavioral cookies.

4. SOLE DATA CONTROLLER (GDPR, CCPA, CPRA & PDPL COMPLIANCE)

All scan records, databases, and generated codes reside exclusively within your device's sandboxed local offline storage. You maintain 100% custody and act as the sole Data Controller of all processed information. You may permanently delete your data at any time via the in-app 'Clear All Records' function or by uninstalling the application.

5. NO SALE OR SHARING OF PERSONAL INFORMATION

We do not sell, rent, disclose, or share any personal information or consumer data with any third parties or data brokers.

6. CHILDREN'S ONLINE PRIVACY (COPPA COMPLIANCE)

Novel QR does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under the age of 16 in the European Union). The Application operates with equal offline privacy protection for users of all ages.
