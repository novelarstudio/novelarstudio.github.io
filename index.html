Featured Project

Novel QR • Professional Barcode Suite Upcoming on Google Play

An independent Android utility engineered for high-precision barcode layout design, standard verification, and local record management. Conforming strictly to international ISO/IEC and GS1 technical specifications, it serves as an offline reference tool for testing, design proofing, and personal productivity with zero internet permissions.

  • 100% Offline Architecture: Engineered with zero internet permissions to guarantee absolute local data confidentiality.
  • 30 Global Standards: Conforms strictly to ISO/IEC and GS1 specifications with exact check-digit algorithms.
  • Zero Advertisements: A focused, distraction-free utility without advertising, subscriptions, or monetization trackers.
  • High-Fidelity Export: Precision vector rendering supporting outputs up to 2400px Ultra HD for physical media proofing.
